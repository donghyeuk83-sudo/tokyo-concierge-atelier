## Rights and venue-photo screening

- **Wikimedia Commons:** the existing [48-place automated candidate search](https://github.com/donghyeuk83-sudo/tokyo-concierge-atelier/actions/runs/39251197345) and human candidate triage in `docs/venue-photo-clearance.md` contain unrelated or other-branch matches, including a French municipality named Sézanne, a different Seirinkan school and a Nagano ninja facility. No discovered candidate qualifies for these Tokyo place IDs solely because its title matches.
- **Flickr original-photo evidence:** `r06` Seirinkan Flickr originals [photo 6225457113](https://www.flickr.com/photos/yusheng/6225457113/) and [photo 2399486554](https://www.flickr.com/photos/marley1/2399486554/) locate the exact Nakameguro restaurant but were marked **All rights reserved**; `r09` Ristorante ASO [photo 18181444855](https://www.flickr.com/photos/iyhon/18181444855) and `r02` Sushi Takeuchi [photo 54190513826](https://www.flickr.com/photos/aktkbk/54190513826/) likewise had no commercial CC reuse grant. New searches did not establish a new exact-branch commercially reusable file. Absence of a search result is not evidence no photograph exists.
- **Press galleries are not a blanket commercial license:** Four Seasons and Mandarin Oriental have press contacts / media image galleries. `r18` and `r21` are **inquiry-only**, not approved media for a separate hotel's public guide. MoonFlower `r50` includes teamLab artworks which may require independent copyright-holder authorization even with restaurant consent.
- Media/application contact forms (e.g. Kura Sushi `r29` / `r37`, Tsukiji Sushiko `r41`, Neo Emotion `r34`) are a particularly suitable first point of contact, but no approval has yet been obtained.
- The old 83 image mappings remain grandfathered for preservation **without claiming they were all independently re-cleared**; the photo-publishing gate rejects new or replaced mappings unless the original file and exact venue/branch permission are documented.

## Permission request checklist

For any approved hotel representative: identify exact original file URL, original photographer or true copyright owner, branch address, commercial hotel concierge guest-guide use, online/public display, cropping and resizing permissions, credit text, geographic scope, duration and revocation conditions. Save the actual written grant and metadata in the venue-specific approval ledger before publishing. Do **not** infer permission from a booking-page image, restaurant gallery, Flickr display page, or CC-licensed image of another store.
