# Guest-facing Tokyo Concierge Atelier photo upgrade · 2026-10-19

This update modifies the **existing site**, not a new website: **131 original venue records preserved, 85 real-photo mappings (83 old + 2 new), 46 missing-photo venues, and 39 external original-publisher photo/venue links** (15 earlier + 24 additions). Existing layout and illustrations remain the fallback. The external links open in a separate tab without embedding unlicensed originals.

- Studio WA `a32` lists an Asakusa area in the original catalogue but official studio material states Meguro; keep picture addition blocked pending correction.
- Permanently closed Katsu Midori Seibu Shibuya `r31` receives no active photo marketing.
- Old audit files with 48 photo-pending venue IDs remain dated **historical research snapshots**. Live `audits/missing-photo-queue.csv` is generated from the actual 85 approved site mappings and now correctly has 46 entries. Tests ensure the 2 removed IDs match the approved files rather than hiding unapproved gaps.
