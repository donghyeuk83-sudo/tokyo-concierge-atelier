## Approval and publication procedure
1. Establish the exact original file and photographer/copyright owner (not merely the site operator).
2. Obtain a matching CC0/CC BY/CC BY-SA original-file grant or explicit **written commercial display/reproduction permission** suitable for this hotel guest guide. Confirm performer/model/property/underlying-artwork releases where required.
3. Confirm photographed location and branch against operator address/floor information. Distinguish historical storefront, building exterior, workshop, and dining room.
4. Record `url`, `source`, `credit`, `license`, `licenseUrl`, `venueEvidenceUrl`, `licenseEvidenceUrl`, `venueIdentityExplanation`, `rightsExplanation` and `reviewedOn` in the clearance ledger **before** any addition to `LICENSED_VENUE_PHOTOS`.
5. Update the original source snapshot deliberately, audit the pending photo queue, verify image naturalWidth and EN/JA/KO at desktop/mobile, and run full Chromium/WebKit, permission checks and exact Pages deployment QA in a PR.
