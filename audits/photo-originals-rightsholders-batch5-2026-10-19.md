## Where to request real permission — 46 published contact routes vs copyright owners

The existing 46 sourced paths are for direct business/media inquiries, **not 46 validated image owners**. Prioritize media/form contacts that can route a written rights request. Rights review must record:

1. Exact photo file URL, original capture date and photographer/copyright owner identity, source license or signed written consent.
2. Exact building/branch and business opening/closure/current address.
3. Explicit commercial hotel concierge website reuse, permitted resizing/crops, required credit, rights in any art/performers depicted, duration and withdrawal procedure.
4. Preserve the actual permission evidence, set the new mapping only after the approval, and test exact deployed image load.
